ukue.com would like to hear from:

- **Anyone trying ukue out.** What worked, what got in the way, and what you used before.
- **Anyone who found a bug.** A job that ran when it shouldn't have, one that didn't run, an error you didn't expect.
- **Anyone with an idea** for something ukue should do, or a language it should have an example for.

Write to **[info@ukue.com](mailto:info@ukue.com)**.

Bug reports are also welcome on [GitHub](https://github.com/ukue-queue/ukue/issues). It helps to include what `ukue version` prints, your operating system, and the steps that show the problem. Please don't send real customer data: a few made-up jobs that show the same thing are enough.
